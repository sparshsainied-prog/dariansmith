Ambassador Ylani has been summoned back to Nilar, where Brannon's parents are accused of murder. If the team can't find the true culprit, the new peace between the two countries crumbles, and with it Brannon's last chance to forgive his parents. Meanwhile Magus Draeson is struggling in his unlikely new role as a parent and mentor.

The investigator behind them

Darian Smith is an Auckland author, counsellor and writing educator, with degrees in Psychology and English and a Diploma of Counselling.

He has worked in family therapy, set up a free community counselling service, and by day works with people living with bleeding disorders such as haemophilia. He says that work shapes his fiction, where complex characters and emotional honesty come first.

He can also be seen on Legend of the Seeker and Spartacus, by those very swift with the pause button. Most Labour Weekends you'll find him at Auckland Armageddon.

Also by Darian: The Lost Pendant of Pania (young adult, 2023), Currents of Change and Shifting Worlds, sixteen stories.
